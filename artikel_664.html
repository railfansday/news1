The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Booster Bundle is now on sale at Walmart — save $15

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Booster Bundle is available for $94.49 at Amazon, but Walmart has it for just $79.99.

Prices for the Pokémon TCG's 30th Celebration Booster Bundle have been holding firm in the $90-$100 price range. Amazon is still there, but sellers are working hard to get you to spend your trading card budget with Walmart.

As of Oct. 16, the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Booster Bundle is available for $79.99 at Walmart, reduced by $15 from its previous $94.99 price. That's nearly 16% off, bringing the cost of each booster pack to around $13.33. This latest offer comes through third-party seller “Unbeatable deals”, with orders still fulfilled by Walmart and free shipping included.

For collectors unfamiliar with the Booster Bundle, it includes six 30th Celebration packs, each containing six holographic cards. Each pack also guarantees one of 30 different Pikachu rare variations, with other sought-after cards across the expansion featuring Mewtwo ex, Mew ex, Umbreon ex, Salamence ex, and Greninja ex.
