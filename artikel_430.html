T-Mobile is giving away the Apple iPhone 17 for free — how to qualify this weekend

TL;DR: The Apple iPhone 17e is free with T-Mobile. To claim this offer, simply switch to T-Mobile on most plans.

We know everyone is obsessing over the iPhone Duo right now. You can even get your hands on Apple's first-ever foldable for $1,000 off this weekend, but that's not what we're here to talk to you about. We're here to discuss free deals.

T-Mobile has been the home of incredible iPhone deals all year. We've been blessed with free giveaways of the iPhone 17 and iPhone 18 Pro in recent times, and even though those deals are no longer live, you can still walk away with an iPhone for free this weekend.

The Apple iPhone 17e is totally free right now with T-Mobile. To qualify for this wild offer, you simply need to switch to T-Mobile on most plans. If you want to upgrade to the iPhone 17, you can get it for under $100 when you bring your number on an Experience More or Experience Beyond plan (no trade-in needed).

The full savings are distributed as monthly credits over a 36-month payment plan. If you leave T-Mobile before the end of the three-year commitment, you will need to pay the remaining balance. You do also need to pay local sales tax on the phone's full retail value upfront, plus a standard $35 device connection fee per line at checkout.

We reviewed the iPhone 17e earlier this year. Stan Schroeder said "The iPhone 17e is a decent upgrade over its predecessor, and a good choice if you want the cheapest new iPhone you can get." If you don't need all the power of the iPhone 18 Pro, it's a solid choice for normal users.

Get the Apple iPhone 17e for free this weekend with T-Mobile.
