Northwell CEO Says Health Care Model Must Change

Northwell Health President and CEO Dr. John D’Angelo tells Bloomberg This Weekend that the traditional US health care model is unsustainable and that improving affordability will require a greater focus on wellness, prevention and chronic disease. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Lisa Mateo, D’Angelo discusses workforce shortages, the financial pressures facing nonprofit hospitals and how AI could reduce administrative burdens while preserving the human role in consequential medical decisions.
