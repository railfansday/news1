The best Disney+ deals and bundles in October 2026

Whether you're looking to jump into the latest superhero saga or journey to a galaxy far, far away, Disney+ is the streaming service for you.

Home to Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, and more, Disney+ offers a library that's stuffed with shows and films to keep both adults and kids entertained. With Disney+, you can watch movies like The Mandalorian and Grogu or Toy Story 5, the new Marvel series VisionQuest, and much more.

The service was just hit with a price hike, but it still offers some great bundle deals if you're hoping to save some cash. Among them is a six-month offer for the ad-supported Disney+ and Hulu bundle that brings the price down to $11.99 per month, then renews at $12.99 per month after that six-month period. Its ad-free plan also includes this deal, dropping the price to $19.99 per month for the first six months, then renewing at $21.99 per month.

There's also the Disney+, Hulu, and HBO Max package, which was unfortunately hit by the price hike as well but is still a great investment for those who already use the other two streaming services and want to cut costs. We've detailed all available bundle deals below, along with basic information on Disney+ plans if you just want the House of Mouse's service on its own.

Disney+ and Hulu Bundle — $11.99 per month for six months, then $12.99 per month

Disney+ and Hulu Bundle Premium — $19.99 per month for six months, then $21.99 per month

The Disney+, Hulu, and HBO Max bundle is one of the best available at the moment. Starting at $21.99 per month, you can have three excellent streaming services right in the palm of your hand. The $21.99 per month option is for the With Ads plan, but if you'd prefer to watch your favorite content without ads, the No Ads plan costs $34.99 per month. Compared to what you'd pay for each of these on their own, you're saving 38% with the ad-supported plan and 43% with the ad-free plan.

Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max Bundle (with ads) — $21.99 per month (save 38%)

Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max Bundle (no ads) — $34.99 per month (save 43%)

The Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle, which has ads, is available for $35.99 per month. The Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN Unlimited premium bundle without ads is available for $44.99 per month.

Disney+ Premium (No Ads) — $21.49 per month, $214.99 per year
