Live: A 'record' 855 people detained in Paris during protests, police say

Police in Paris said Sunday they had detained a "record figure" of 855 people in the capital during the demonstrations that swept France the day before. The police prefecture added that 13 police officers and gendarmes had been injured during the clashes that broke out on the margins of the mass demonstrations.
