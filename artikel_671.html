These Products Are Pink for Breast Cancer Awareness Month & Give Back

Shop these beauty, fashion and home pieces during Breast Cancer Awareness Month that all give back to causes supporting treatment and research for breast cancer.

October marks Breast Cancer Awareness Month, an annual campaign to support the millions affected by breast cancer. With approximately 1 in 8 women in the U.S. being diagnosed annually with breast cancer, according to the American Cancer Society, you can give back in meaningful, stylish ways through brand collaborations and limited-edition product releases that support treatment and research.

Many of our favorite beauty, fashion and home brands are giving back this year to a number of organizations, and we rounded them up for an thoughtful way to show your support through the rest of October:

From pink blankets for staying cozy this winter to beauty products and tools, chic jewelry pieces and more, these 10+ brands are giving back, all month long.

Customize select Emilie and Everlyne styles by Kendra Scott with Rose Quartz, and 20% of all sales will support the Breast Cancer Research Foundation through the Kendra Scott Foundation.

25% of the purchase price from items in Ralph Lauren's Pink Pony collection will benefit programs for cancer screenings, early treatment, research and patient navigation.

$25 of the purchase price for this kit, which features a range of treatments and products, will be donated to the Tides Foundation, benefitting Memorial Sloan Kettering's Cancer Center for breast cancer immunotherapy research. A minimum donation of $350,000 will be given to the center.

100% of sales of the Pink Tie Dye blanket, which features a soft tie-dye blend of blush, rose and cream, will be donated to The Huntsman Cancer Institute.

20% of the Breast Cancer Awareness Collection by TELETIES will benefit Breast Cancer Care at AdventHealth.

25% of net sales from every Pop Pink LUXE+ device will be donated to the Breast Cancer Research Foundation (BCRF).

At Hotel Lobby Candle, $5 from every Signature Candle purchase will be donated to the Breast Cancer Research Foundation (BCRF).

In partnership with cancer survivor Fran Drescher's Cancer Schmancer Initiative, 50% of proceeds from this kit (containing Hydrating Blush, Hydrating Lip Color & Brush) goes toward Cancer Schmancer.

50% of the purchase price of gorjana's Pink Collection directly benefits the Breast Cancer Research Foundation (BCRF).

La Bonne Brosse's limited-edition Pink October collection directly contributes to research funded by the  Breast Cancer Research Foundation (BCRF).

20% of all proceeds from stakt's Rosewater products will be donated to The Pink Agenda, helping to raise awareness and fund research.

CAKES Body's BCAM Bundle, featuring the Grippy CAKES circles, Sticky CAKES circles and a Breast Health insert, will donate 30% of revenue (up to $50,000) to the Breast Cancer Research Foundation (BCRF). The brand has also pledged a minimum annual donation of $250,000 to BCRF by June 2027.

In honor of Breast Cancer Awareness Month, 50% from each of ChappyWrap's Pink Ribbon Edition blanket sold will be donated to the American Cancer Society.
