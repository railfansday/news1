Free People’s Holiday Shop Is Open—Cozy, Boho Pieces We’d Buy First

From cozy knitted accessories to party-ready pieces, these are our favorite finds from the Free People holiday shop.

Free People officially launched its holiday shop. Filled with so many cute and soft items, the shop has everything you could possibly need for the upcoming holiday season.

We scoured through the collection and picked our absolute favorites (quilted onesies, cozy legwarmers, and knitted bandanas, witchy tarot card ornaments, anyone?).

Whether you're buying for yourself or you're already working on what to gift everyone in your life, keep scrolling to shop our favorite styles from Free People's Holiday collection.
