PSA: The Dyson Airwrap i.d. is $195 off at Best Buy in this better-than-Black Friday clearance deal

SAVE $195: As of Oct. 16, the Dyson Airwrap i.d. Multi-Styler for Curly + Coily Hair is on clearance at Best Buy for only $454.99. That's 30% off its suggested retail price of $649.99.

If you're planning on gifting a Dyson Airwrap this holiday season (to a loved one or just yourself), heed my advice: Don't bother putting off your purchase until Black Friday rolls around. Best Buy currently has the special-edition "amber silk" colorway of the Airwrap i.d. for Curly + Coily Hair marked down to only $454.99 as part of a clearance deal. That's a very rare 30% savings.

For some added context, Airwrap discounts usually max out around $150 during the holidays. I should also point out that another model geared toward straight and wavy hair has already sold out at Best Buy at its clearance price of $552.99. (And that's a way worse deal!)

Launched in 2024, the Airwrap i.d. is a mid-tier variant of Dyson's legendary multi-styler with several different attachments and Bluetooth support — meaning you can use the MyDyson mobile app to program a custom hair routine. It was re-released in amber silk for the holidays last year, so I'm assuming Best Buy is just getting rid of its remaining inventory to make way for this season's new limited-edition "plum and copper" finish.

The Curly + Coily version of the Airwrap i.d. comes with conical barrel, diffuser, volumizing brush, loop brush, long barrel, fast dryer, and detangling comb attachments (plus a storage case and filter-cleaning brush).
