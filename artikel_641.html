Kate Hudson Shows Off Abs in Red Bikini During Beach Workout

Kate Hudson gave fans a peek at how she achieves her abs as she rocked a red bikini while on vacation in Hawaii.

It's safe to say it took Kate Hudson much longer than 10 days to achieve her rock-solid abs.

The How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days star showed off her toned tummy while enjoying a Pilates workout on the beach during her luxurious Hawaiian getaway.

In a video shared to her Instagram Oct. 16, Hudson shared moments from her weekend celebrating pal Jennifer Meyer’s wedding to billionaire Geoff Ogunlesi on the island of Lanai, including a clip where the Running Point actress proved she doesn’t need a gym to get in a good workout.

Indeed, the 47-year-old—donning a red-hot bandeau bikini and sunglasses—could be seen getting her sweat on as she did a few sit-ups on her towel while lounging with friends by the ocean.

But that was just part of the pre-wedding festivities. After all, Hudson also included a close-up of her vibrant wedding guest look, which was composed of a shimmering gold gown and statement jewelry.

As for her wedding date, Hudson was joined by her fiancé Danny Fujikawa, who accompanied her to the extravagant ceremony wearing a classic black tuxedo.

“What a time!” she captioned the post. “To love, to laughter, and to never leaving Hawaii Congrats to the stunning bride and groom.”

And while she was ecstatic to witness her friends get hitched, the Bride Wars actress—who shares daughter Rani, 8, with Fujikawa; son Bingham, 15, with ex-fiancé Matt Bellamy; and son Ryder, 22, with ex-husband Chris Robinson—recently shared why she’s in no hurry to tie the knot herself, despite getting engaged to the Chief alum in 2021.

"That's part of why I don't want to get married—it's too expensive," she shared on the Sept. 8 episode of her Sibling Revelrey podcast. "I just want hors d'oeuvres. I wanna do, like, chips and salsa."

Another holdup for the Almost Famous actress—who was raised by mom Goldie Hawn and stepdad Kurt Russell—is the fact that she has "such a huge family" and would struggle to whittle down the guest list.

"I still am not married. I'm just engaged, and I keep thinking about wedding, like what are we gonna do," Hudson explained. "And then every time we start thinking about who we have to invite, Danny's like, 'I just want a small wedding.' I'm like, ‘Our family alone is like 50 people.’"

As she put it, "There's a million kids. There's a million uncles and aunts and cousins."
