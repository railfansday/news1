Porsche launched a car on flying Tesla day and the memes are incredible

Picking when to launch a shiny new product is half the battle.

You've got to make sure the timing is right, and the absolute last thing you want is to get overshadowed by a competitor.

Enter Porsche. On Thursday, the company unveiled a new supercar called the Mission S, sharing a post on X and describing it as "a glimpse into the future."

Unfortunately for them, on the exact same day Tesla had its own launch — and their car could fly.

Unsurprisingly, the timing didn't go unnoticed.

And it wasn't long before the memes started.
