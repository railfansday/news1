Khloe Kardashian Reveals What She'd Name Her 3rd Baby

Khloe Kardashian—who shares kids True, 8, and Tatum, 4, with ex Tristan Thompson—said it would be "cute" to name her third baby Tyla, a moniker suggested by her oldest daughter.

Khloe Kardashian is keeping up with naming traditions.

In fact, the reality star already has name in mind that'll match the monikers of her and ex Tristan Thompson's kids True, 8, and Tatum, 4, should she ever have a third baby.

“I actually love the name, so I think I want to use this name if I ever do it: Tyla," Khloe told mom Kris Jenner on the Oct. 16 of The Kardashians. "Tyla, True, Tatum—how cute!"

But the 42-year-old can't take all the credit for coming up with the name. After all, it was initially True's idea after finding out that her mom still has a few frozen embryos in storage, which Khloe said she "owns outright" after striking a legal agreement with Tristan.

"I was on the phone and I was talking to the doctor. I said out loud, ‘She has a sister in the freezer,’" Khloe explained. "So now True is laser focused that she has a sister in the freezer. She’s told people."

However, the Khloe in Wonder Land podcaster doesn't know if she has "the bandwidth" to expand her family at this stage in life.

"I do believe every girl needs a sister. I personally would love another daughter," Khloe shared in a confessional. "But also, I’m getting older and it’s a lot. I don’t know if this is something I can handle."

That's why Khloe—who admittedly has been celibate for nearly five years—believes the "ideal" situation for her is to "be with someone else and not necessarily have kids with that person."

"I don't want me and my husband to have kids, and then my kids feel, 'Oh, we're not as important because their daddy lives in this house,'" she continued. "I don't want to cause a lot of disruption within my household because I love the way it is."

But while Kris is reluctant to believe that "Prince Charming" would be fine with Khloe having "a baby in the freezer with [her] ex-boyfriend," the Good American founder does have a plan.

"I'm gonna say, 'The surrogate is pregnant,'" she told her mom, "or I'm not going to have another baby."

Khloe added, "Marriage, you can divorce and if you don't have kids, you don't have to see them again. When you have a baby, it's forever. So, choose widely."
