GTA 6 soundtrack: Every song, artist, and celebrity DJ we know about so far

What's a Grand Theft Auto game without a killer soundtrack?

From the sun-soaked synth-pop of Vice City to surfing a sea of radio stations, music has always been an essential part of Rockstar Games' gritty, open-world franchise. And with Grand Theft Auto VI taking players back to Vice City, the sprawling soundtrack is shaping up to be just as ambitious as the game itself.

Rockstar has already revealed a 34-track album featuring artists like Travis Scott, Cardi B, and PinkPantheress, along with an original score composed by the French electronic duo Justice. Meanwhile, the game's fictional radio stations are bringing some familiar voices to the airwaves, including Robyn, Bad Bunny, and Lana Del Rey.

And that's just what we know so far. Ahead of GTA 6's Nov. 19 release, we're keeping track of every confirmed song, artist, radio station, and celebrity DJ coming to Leonida. Consider this your unofficial guide to the sounds of Vice City.

Rockstar has announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, a 34-track collection of original music arriving Nov. 19, the same day as the game.

The album features an eclectic mix of artists and genres, from hip-hop heavyweights Travis Scott and Cardi B to country star Morgan Wallen and Puerto Rican singer Rauw Alejandro. There's even a collaboration between CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.., and French producer Etienne de Crécy. And somehow, someway, Morgan Wallen's single is the most-streamed track from the soundtrack on Spotify, with more than 25 million streams. Yung Lean, fight back!

Here are the nine songs Rockstar has revealed from the album so far:

"That's It" — Yung Lean feat. Future & Metro Boomin

"Sexy Magic" — CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. & Etienne de Crécy

"Flower in a Hurricane" — Justice feat. Tame Impala, SZA & King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard

"Bright Lights, Big City" — Keith Richards

Fans can listen to the available singles on Rockstar's official GTA 6 music website, where the album is also available to pre-save or pre-order on vinyl and CD.

The French electronic duo Justice, consisting of Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay, is responsible for the original score of Grand Theft Auto VI.

Rockstar confirmed the news on Oct. 15 alongside the game's launch trailer, which features the debut of Justice's new single, "Flower in a Hurricane," featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and Australian psychedelic rock band King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.

It's a fitting collaboration for a game returning to Vice City, a fictional playground known for its vibrant nightlife and electronic music. Justice's signature blend of distorted synths, disco, and dance music feels particularly suited to Rockstar's radiant criminal underworld.

The duo previously collaborated with Tame Impala on "Neverender" from their 2024 album Hyperdrama. The song won Best Dance/Electronic Recording at the 2025 Grammys.

Rockstar has also confirmed that an official score album is on the way, although additional details, including its release date and tracklist, have yet to be announced.

The fictional radio stations are one of Grand Theft Auto's most beloved features, and GTA 6 is bringing some major star power to the airwaves.

On Oct. 8, Rockstar unveiled six radio stations, each with its own musical identity and celebrity hosts. Among the biggest names are Bad Bunny, Robyn, Lana Del Rey, Burna Boy, and members of Slayer.

Here's a breakdown of the confirmed stations and their hosts:

Bad Bunny is bringing the sounds of Puerto Rico to Vice City. Alongside singer RaiNao, the superstar hosts Cocoteo FM, a station dedicated to modern Latin music, including reggaeton and other Caribbean sounds.

A familiar name for longtime Vice City fans, Flash FM returns with Swedish pop icon Robyn and model Alex Consani behind the microphone. The station promises a steady stream of pop music and dance-floor favorites.

Country music gets its own corner of Leonida with Back Country Radio, hosted by Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey. In perhaps the most unexpected casting choice, Del Rey's husband, Jeremy Dufrene, also makes an appearance as the station's "roving reporter, swamp meteorologist, and airboat captain."

Afrobeats superstar Burna Boy teams up with DJ and producer Palms Trax for a station spotlighting amapiano, African classics, and other sounds from across the continent.

Metal fans aren't being left out. Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Araya host The Chamber, a station dedicated to heavy metal.

Miami rap legends Trick Daddy and Trina are taking listeners through the history of Southern hip-hop on Dirty South Classics, a particularly fitting addition to a game inspired by South Florida.

These are only the stations Rockstar has revealed so far. The developer says GTA 6 will feature its biggest and most wide-ranging collection of in-game radio stations and podcasts yet, with everything from classic rock to talk radio.

Long before Rockstar began revealing the official soundtrack, the game's trailers were already giving fans a taste of what to expect musically.

The first GTA 6 trailer, released in December 2023, featured Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," a track from his 1989 album Full Moon Fever. The song was an especially fitting choice given Petty's Florida roots and the game's focus on Jason and Lucia's relationship.

The Oct. 15, 2026, launch trailer introduced "Flower in a Hurricane," the new Justice collaboration featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.

Rockstar has launched an official music hub where fans can preview the game's radio stations and listen to singles from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album.

The album arrives Nov. 19, 2026, alongside the game, with digital, vinyl, and CD editions planned.

As Rockstar reveals more music, we'll update this guide with additional songs, artists, radio stations, and soundtrack details.
