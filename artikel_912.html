Anton Stepnov and colleagues rightly highlight concerns about using common laboratory tests to study plastic-eating microorganisms (see A. A. Stepnov et al. Nature 657, 611–620; 2026). However, extending their discussion to the fate of particles shed during whole-cell experiments raises a crucial question: when the mass of a sample decreases, has the plastic truly been removed?
