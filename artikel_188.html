Helen Duncan and the Spiritualist Trial of the Century

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

A detailed history of Helen Duncan, the Scottish medium whose wartime séances, claims about the sinking of HMS Barham, and 1944 arrest led to one of Britain’s most famous witchcraft trials. It traces her rise in spiritualism, exposures by skeptics, the police sting that caught her in Portsmouth, and the court case that turned her into a cause célèbre.
