Sharon Osbourne Says Plastic Surgery "Mistake" Left Dent in Her Face

Sharon Osbourne revealed a scary experience from going under the knife, sharing the procedure left her with an “indentation” in her face.

Sharon Osbourne is sharing insight into a botched cosmetic procedure.

The Talk alum detailed one of her plastic surgeries gone wrong, revealing that she woke up with a “dent” in her face.

When her son Jack Osbourne asked on the Oct. 15 episode of The Osbournes podcast if she’s ever felt “scared” about undergoing a procedure, she replied, “I have when I’ve woken up and seen the mistakes.”

“One time, it was this color black, all the way down here,” she told Jack and his wife Aree Gearhart, gesturing to her face, making a line between her upper lip to her chin. “I have an indentation left from it.”

As for what went wrong, the 74-year-old said she believes “the surgeon left it to his mate to do” the procedure.

“You go and complain, and then he starts injecting,” Sharon continued, “and you go, ‘What is in this that you’re injecting me with?’ and they go, ‘Don’t worry. It’s my special concoction. It’ll work.’”

“Well, actually it didn’t f--king work,” she added. “I’ve got a dent!”

While Jack and Aree assured the X Factor alum the mark isn’t very noticeable, she admitted that it’s ultimately on her for choosing to go under the knife.

“It’s not like I earn my living out of my f--king face,” Sharon noted, “so it’s like, OK, I f--ked up, my choice, that’s it.”

And though the British television personality has been open about her many plastic surgeries over the years, it appears she may be finished with getting work done.

When Jack asked Sharon if she’s “done with the face,” she replied, “Oh Jack. Who am I doing it for now?”

Along with wanting to avoid the “brutal” recovery process, Sharon noted that she wants her body to appear cohesive.

“What am I going to do? You have your face done. You've got a tight face,” she explained. “Well, look at my hands. Look at my arms. I never show my arms because they're wrinkly and marked. The skin’s going.”

“It’s like, what about the rest of you?” she added. “And then you’ve got a face that looks 50, and your body looks your age.”

As for her mindset now, Sharon emphasized, “Go with it.”
