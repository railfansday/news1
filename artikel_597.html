Choosing a Theragun is less about finding the model with the most features and more about figuring out which features you'll actually use. Think about how and where you'll use it, then consider:

How often you'll use it: Regular recovery or occasional soreness?

Where you'll use it: Mostly at home or at the gym and while traveling?

Size, weight and grip: Consider portability, how comfortable it is to hold and whether you can reach areas like your back and shoulders.

Intensity and speeds: Look at how much control you have over the massage.

Attachments: Different attachments can target different muscles and areas of the body.

Noise level: This may matter if you plan to use it around other people.

Battery life: Frequent users and travelers may want longer stretches between charges.

Budget: Consider which features matter enough to pay more for.
