When Joseph Stiglitz and colleagues wrote in Nature why the world needs an International Panel on Inequality (see Nature 657, 345–348; 2026), they added one more topic to the list of global issues for which advocates find inspiration from the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC). The IPCC has been proposed as providing a template for authoritative knowledge assessments of a range of issues, such as antimicrobial resistance, migration, pandemics, food systems and artificial-intelligence governance.
