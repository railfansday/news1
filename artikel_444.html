The holidays are coming — and a Sam’s Club Plus membership comes with all the perks

TL;DR: Get a one-year Sam’s Club Plus Membership with Auto-Renew for $120 and make holiday shopping a little easier.

The holiday season has a way of turning a simple shopping list into a full-blown project. Between festive dinners, gifts, decorations, and everyday essentials, having a single place to handle multiple errands can make a difference. A one-year Sam’s Club Plus Membership with Auto-Renew is $120, making now an ideal time to join before the seasonal rush begins.

At Sam’s Club, members can shop for groceries, kitchen supplies, electronics, furniture, and holiday decorations, along with household staples worth keeping stocked year-round.

The Plus membership adds perks that can make shopping more convenient, especially during busy weeks.

Early shopping hours let Plus members get a head start before regular club hours. Qualifying in-club purchases also earn 2 percent back in Sam’s Cash, up to $500 annually (subject to program terms).

Prefer shopping from home? Plus members can get free shipping on eligible online orders and complimentary curbside pickup.

Membership benefits extend beyond groceries and gifts. Members can access fuel savings at select locations, while Plus perks include pharmacy savings on hundreds of generic medications and optical discounts.

Vehicle-related services include complimentary flat-tire repair, battery testing, and wiper-blade installation.

You’ll also receive a complimentary household card for another eligible household member to shop. For families planning holiday gatherings, the combination of members-only prices, convenient pickup, and early access can help simplify seasonal shopping. And these benefits aren’t just for December. They can be useful throughout the year for groceries, household essentials, and everyday errands.

Get a one-year Sam’s Club Plus Membership with Auto-Renew for $120 and start planning your holiday shopping.
