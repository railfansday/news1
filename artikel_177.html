The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is (almost) free this weekend — how to claim

TL;DR: The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is $1,000 off with T-Mobile. To claim this offer, switch and bring your number or trade in an eligible phone in any condition on an Experience Beyond plan.

The iPhone Duo is now available to preorder. That's obviously the focus right now, but you can actually score the iPhone 18 Pro for a ridiculously low price if you don't mind missing out on Apple's first foldable.

For a limited time, the Apple iPhone 18 Pro is available for under $200 when you switch and bring your number or trade in an eligible phone in any condition on an Experience Beyond plan.

The full savings are distributed as monthly credits over a 36-month payment plan. If you leave T-Mobile before the end of the three-year commitment, you will be charged the remaining balance. That's how they get you. You're paying a super-low upfront cost, but you're committing to stick it out with T-Mobile.

You do need to pay local sales tax on the phone's full retail value upfront, plus a standard $35 device connection fee per line at checkout. That's something you simply can't escape with this type of mobile carrier deal.
