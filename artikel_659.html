The Ultimate Preppy Sneaker for Your Fall Wardrobe

The K-Swiss x Lucky in Love sneakers bringing preppy collegiate vibes to your fall wardrobe with crisp white and forest green details.

Preppy style is having a moment, and E! has found the sneakers that offer classic tennis-club vibes, collegiate cool, and the kind of timeless appeal that makes even a simple jeans-and-sweater outfit look put together.

Rooted in Ivy League campuses and country-club fashion, prep has always been about polished staples with a sporty twist. And you don’t need a closet full of cable-knit sweaters and pleated skirts to get the look.

The K-Swiss x Lucky in Love Hypercourt Express 3 sneakers bring together two tennis brands for a fresh take on court-inspired style, featuring a crisp white design with forest green accents that feels practically made for fall.

The color combination is classic, versatile, and easy to wear straight through winter, whether you’re pairing these sneakers with low-slung denim, a mini skirt and crew socks, or your favorite cozy layers.

The K-Swiss x Lucky in Love sneakers are giving major preppy vibes with their crisp white design and forest green accents. These limited-edition kicks are perfect for pairing with everything you'll wear this fall.

It also has a lightweight, cushioned design with breathable mesh and a comfy fit that doesn’t require a break-in period.
