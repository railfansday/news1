The Sunseeker X3 Plus robot lawn mower has dropped to its best-ever price — save over $700

SAVE OVER $700: As of Oct. 16, the Sunseeker X3 Plus robot lawn mower has hit its lowest-ever price of $836.98 at Amazon. That's $762.01 off its full price of $1,598.99.

As we inch closer to winter, outdoor work like mowing the lawn starts winding down. However, there's nothing wrong with planning ahead for next year's yard chores, and Amazon can help by offering stellar discounts on select robot lawn mowers right now. Among them is the Sunseeker X3 Plus, which is nearly half off.

As of Oct. 16, this robot mower has plummeted from $1,598.99 to $836.98, saving you 48% overall. It also marks the lowest price we've seen for the mower, which is a huge bonus.

The Sunseeker X3 Plus is designed for yards in a small-to-medium size range. That means lawns up to 0.3 acres/13,000 sq. ft. There's no need to bury perimeter wires, as you can create virtual boundaries in the app. When it comes to cutting, it features an offset blade design which helps it trim closer to fences and borders, and cutting height can even be adjusted in the app. Plus, it can handle slopes up to 30 degrees, so if your yard has a few bumps, it won't be slowed down.

This mower even features a camera and ultrasonic sensor that help it find and maneuver around objects on the lawn.

If the Sunseeker X3 Plus has caught your eye, act fast to save at Amazon.
