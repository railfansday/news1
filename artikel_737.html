Jon Gosselin Defends Son Collin Gosselin Over "Heartbreaking" Memoir

After Kate Gosselin agreed that her son Collin Gosselin is “mentally ill” amid the release of his memoir In the Shadow of Eight, Jon Gosselin clapped back: “I couldn’t be more proud.”

Warning: This article contains allegations of child abuse.

Jon Gosselin is speaking out in support of his son.

Three days after Collin Gosselin released his tell-all memoir In the Shadow of Eight: Surviving the Reality of My Childhood, in which he accused his mom Kate Gosselin of verbal and physical abuse in his youth, his dad fiercely defended the book.

“First and foremost I couldn’t be more proud of my son’s heartbreaking memoir and his mission to help other victims of abuse,” Jon told E! News in a statement. “Also, let me be very clear: if you continue to falsely label my son Collin as ‘mentally ill’ you will be held accountable.”

Following the release of Collin’s memoir, one social media user claimed that he was "encouraged and enabled by other dysfunctional and mentally ill people" to write the book, to which Kate responded, “Exactly! I had nothing to do with it, except for consent because it was medically necessary.”

However, Jon—who was awarded custody of Collin in 2018 upon his release from a mental health facility, which Kate had sent him to for what she previously described as “his own well-being”—pushed back on those labels.

“As you know, Collin has been evaluated by multiple independent psychiatrists, and that narrative is proven categorically untrue,” the 49-year-old continued in his statement to E!. “Repeating false claims about his mental health is damaging to his reputation, his credibility, and his future.”

He added, “Consider this fair warning: the days of using mental health to discredit and silence my son are over.”

In the book, Collin—one of Jon and Kate’s sextuplets—described his experience at the in-patient facility where he was allegedly medicated and physically restrained during a 21-month stay. The 22-year-old also alleged that Kate would physically punish him as a child.

“When my mom was angry, she would grab me by the ear,” he wrote. “Sometimes it ended there. Sometimes it didn’t. A snappy pull could turn into a smack to the side of my head, like a punctuation mark.”

But while he may be estranged from Kate these days, Collin emphasized there’s an opportunity for reconciliation, but only if effort is made.

"I'm a big believer that actions speak louder than words," he recently told E! News. "And while we can say what we want to say, you have to back that up. And there's a lot of accountability that has to be taken on her part.”

He added, “I've done my due diligence, I've put everything out there, and she's yet to come forward and take accountability for what she's done."

For all the bombshells from Collin’s new memoir, keep reading…

Collin Gosselin Says His Mom Separated Him From His Siblings

On a 2015 episode of Kate Plus 8—the Jon & Kate Plus 8 spinoff Kate Gosselin  led following her 2009 divorce from Jon Gosselin—TLC viewers watched as twins Mady Gosselin and Cara Gosselin moved into their own rooms in Kate's 7,500-square-foot estate, while their younger sisters Leah Gosselin, Alexis Gosselin and Hannah Gosselin inhabited a shared upstairs room and brothers Collin Gosselin, Joel Gosselin and Aaden Gosselin made over a basement space.

"It was like a slumber party every night," Collin recounts of that first week in his memoir Shadow of Eight: Surviving the Reality of My Childhood. "Then, one day, I was told by my mom I’d be moving out. An air mattress appeared in the unfinished part of the basement, shoved into the far back corner like an afterthought."

Coming on the heels of him being cut out of weekend visits with his dad (he was told Jon didn't want to see him; Jon was told the opposite) and frequently isolated (he recalls one night where he was sent to bed while his siblings watched TV with Kate), having to live next to their deep freezers felt particularly cold.

"I was part of a plan," he notes of the punishments, he attributed to his unwillingness to play ball on camera "I just didn’t know what it was."

E! News reached out to reps for Kate and TLC about the accusations in Collin's book and has not heard back. However, this past June, Kate referred to her son's accusations as "ridiculous" in the comment section of her TikTok page.

Collin Gosselin Claims His Parents Kate Gosselin and Jon Gosselin Were Offered Millions to Stay Married

As Jon and Kate's marriage began to unravel, claims Collin, Dr. Phil host Phil McGraw was teaming with TLC "to negotiate a deal to keep their marriage together for cameras."

So, when the couple shared their plans to divorce in January 2009, writes Collin, "the network responded by offering my parents $2 million to stay in the marriage and pretend the relationship was intact for the sake of the show’s longevity." Turning down the offer, Jon told the Daily Mail in 2019, "I couldn't live with myself. I couldn't live a lie."

E! News reached out to reps for Kate and TLC, but they did not respond. They also declined to comment on Daily Mail's previous story. A rep for McGraw called the allegations "false" in a statement to Us Weekly. Noting the talk show host didn't have "any direct or indirect interest, financial or otherwise," in the series, the rep added, "Neither Dr. Phil, nor anyone associated with his show ever facilitated, encouraged, participated in or knew anything about any payments to the Gosselins by the other network as alleged by their son Collin, who Dr. Phil has never met."

Collin Gosselin Says He Spent 70 Percent of His Time Locked in His Basement Bedroom

According to Collin, some 14 complaints were made against his mom to Children and Youth Services, one of them coming after he turned up to his fourth-grade classroom with a noticeably injured ear.

Not long after, "my mom pulled me from school," he wrote. "She told me it was temporary. That it was for my own good. That people were 'asking questions' and we needed to keep things calm for a while. I didn’t argue or ask how long. I'd learned that asking questions made things worse."

Collin said he was also banned from playing with the family's LEGO sets. But perhaps the biggest change, Collin alleged, was the walls Kate had built around his basement air mattress with two security cameras he had to speak through if he needed to be let out to use the bathroom.

"Most days, I spent about 70 percent of my time locked in that room," writes Collin. "The rest was split between meeting with my teacher, eating alone without my siblings, cleaning up after everyone or pretending to be happy when the cameras came around. That was my life."

Collin Gosselin Claims Mom Kate Gosselin Told His Siblings He Was a Bad Kid

To explain why Collin was no longer attending school with them or even joining the rest of the family to eat dinner, Kate would tell his siblings he was troubled, Collin writes.

"'Collin needs more help than you kids,' she would say right in front of me," he details in his book. "'You and your friends at school aren't bad like your brother.' They were repeatedly told that I was unpredictable, violent, beyond help. They grew up around me as if I was a cautionary tale. They were children themselves. How could they know to doubt her?"

Kate previously told People that Collin received "multiple psychiatric diagnoses" and inpatient psychiatric treatment in response to "unpredictable and violent behavior." But Jon claimed to the Daily Mail in 2019 that Collin had no known diagnosed medical condition other than common ADHD.

Collin Gosselin Alleges Mom Kate Gosselin Once Bound His Wrists and Ankles With Zip Ties

Having tired of his basement existence by age 11, Collin plotted his escape, using his twin-size air mattress to climb out the tiny basement window. But with nowhere to go—"It had been almost two years since I'd seen my dad"—he returned home.

Once there, his mattress was gone, as were the few books he had. Then, Collin detailed, Kate allegedly came in with zip ties for his wrists and ankles.

"Once I was bound, she grabbed me by my wrists, pulled me up into a standing position and then threw me down, slamming my tailbone against the hard floor," Collin alleges, adding she told him he was a burden to the family. "She went on, calling me crazy, dangerous and other names nobody should ever call a child, things that stuck long after the pain faded."

A rep for Kate didn’t respond to E! News' request for comment. But her attorney previously told The Sun, "There's always two sides to every story and when things take place in the past, it's hard to determine because people have differing recollections and perspectives."

However, his take, he added, was "if Kate did the things that Collin is accusing Kate of, she would have been investigated by the authorities and prosecuted."

Collin Gosselin Recounts Being Physically Restrained and Sedated at a Mental Health Clinic

After his escape attempt, Collin claims he was transported to Fairmount Behavioral Health System mere days before his 12th birthday. Throughout his 21-month stay in the mental health clinic—where most kids stayed just days or weeks, he claims—he was given daily medication. "My brain had started feeling soft and cloudy, like someone had turned a dimmer switch down," he details. "I found myself zoning out, feeling like a zombie."

During one incident, 18 months into his stay, Collin alleges he was physically restrained and injected with a medication he dubbed "booty juice" after asking administrators why he was still there. After another, a staffer bent his wrist in such a way that "a violent, searing pain shot up my arm, so intense it made my knees buckle." (E! News reached out to Fairmount Behavioral Health System, but has not heard back.)

Throughout his nearly two-year stay, Collin says his mom visited once.

Collin Gosselin Says He Smuggled a Letter to Dad Jon Gosselin to Get Out of the Mental Health Clinic

With his mom and siblings no longer taking his calls from Fairmount, said Collin, he hatched a plan to reconnect with his dad. After hitting it off with one of his roommates, Collin confided in him about his reality star past.

Writing a letter to his father in crayon while hiding in a bathroom stall, Collin claimed he asked his roommate to find a way to deliver it. Soon after, he was discharged to a residential facility where he was able to once-again attend school and slowly weaned off his medications.

It was there that he connected with his dad for the first time, Jon sharing in that initial phone call that he had plans to bring him home.

As Jon later detailed to Collin—who ended up moving in with him, sister Hannah and Jon's then-girlfriend Colleen Conrad—he had spent years trying to track him down. Then he received Collin's letter and filed an emergency petition with the court to modify custody.

Collin Gosselin Says He Hasn't Seen Most of His Siblings Since He Was 15

Other than Hannah, Collin has only seen his siblings once since leaving Kate's home at age 11—a court-mandated visit just before his 15th birthday. "It was a way to keep the kids together, despite being split between two households," Collin reasons, noting the meet-up started out awkwardly before they all teamed up to best the amusement center's games.

But, as Leah, Alexis, Joel and Aaden moved toward the exit, "No one turned back," Collin recounts. "And I haven't seen them since."
