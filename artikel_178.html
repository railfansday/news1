A New 'Super Ice' Is 10 Times Stronger, Bringing It Close to the Strength of Concrete

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

Researchers created BioPykrete, an ice composite reinforced with cellulose nanocrystals and an antifreeze-protein-based glue. In lab tests, it was about 10 times stronger than regular ice and absorbed far more energy before breaking, raising the possibility of low-carbon building materials for remote cold regions.
