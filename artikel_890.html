"VisionQuest" is as much about memory as it is Vision, so densely is MCU lore woven into its worlds-within-worlds plot.

At its heart, it's about Vision's identity crisis. He remains hopelessly devoted to an unreachable love, in thrall to Ultron, and at odds with these memories that will never truly be his own – and that's if they're even real to begin with.

But it's not all exposition and musings on existence, because Paladin is hired by an unknown kingpin to track Vision down so that his technological assets can be stolen.

There are multiple "VisionQuest" trailers, which are sure to jog your memory in different ways.

The show is heavily informed not only by 2021's "WandaVision" but also by 2015's "Avengers: Age of Ultron" and 2024's "Agatha All Along", all three of which may be worth revisiting before embarking on "VisionQuest".
