Academy Museum Gala 2026 Red Carpet: Taylor Swift, Inde Navarrette, Kate Hudson and More

The red carpet at the Academy Museum Gala is now open.

Once again, the event is proving to be one of Hollywood’s most glamorous A-list nights of the year.

Among those expected to walk the carpet are Taylor Swift, Connor Storrie, Jessica Biel and Justin Timberlake, Kim Kardashian, Jacob Elordi, Kendall Jenner, Eva Longoria, Salma Hayek, Inde Navarrette, Nia Long, McKenna Grace, Rosé, Sabrina Carpenter, Drew Starkey, Janelle Monáe, Pamela Anderson, Charles Melton, Alexander Skarsgård, Tom Ford and many others.

The program for the sixth annual gala, which takes place at and benefits the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures, includes honoring Charlize Theron with the Icon Award, Colman Domingo with the Vantage Award and John Carpenter with the Luminary Award.

Swift is set to be this year’s performer.

The gala, presented by Rolex, is co-chaired by Robert Rodriguez, Steven Spielberg and Kate Capshaw.

The host committee includes Jessica Alba, Judd Apatow and Leslie Mann, Autumn Durald Arkapaw, Adrien Brody and Georgina Chapman, Jon M. Chu, Kirsten Dunst, Nicholas Galitzine, Ludwig and Serena Göransson, Regina Hall, Kate Hudson, Jessica Lange, Greta Lee, John Legend and Chrissy Teigen, Rami Malek, Demi Moore, Olivia Munn, Gina Prince-Bythewood, Questlove, Da’Vine Joy Randolph, Seth Rogen and Lauren Miller Rogen, Bird Runningwater and Teyana Taylor.
