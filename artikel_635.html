Harry Potter's Bonnie Wright Welcomes Baby No. 2 With Andrew Lococo

Bonnie Wright, who played Ginny Weasley in Harry Potter, welcomed her second baby with husband Andrew Lococo Sept. 28: “Thank you universe for my loving bundle of boys!”

UPDATE: Bonnie Wright is stepping into the next chapter of her wizarding world of motherhood.

The Harry Potter alum and husband Andrew Lococo—who share 3-year-old son Elio—shared they’ve welcomed their second baby together, a little boy named Aster Sky Wright.

“Say hello to our son Aster Sky Wright Lococo born at home on the 28th September named after the star-shaped flower,” Wright, 35, wrote on Instagram Oct. 17 featuring photos of their newborn. “Our little star has been showered in love by his big brother Elio. Thank you universe for my loving bundle of boys!

Bonnie Wright is experiencing the magic of motherhood once again.

The Harry Potter alum, 35, announced she is pregnant with her second baby in a heartwarming Instagram post on Easter Sunday. Bonnie shared the news alongside two photos of her son, Elio Ocean Wright Lococo, 2, whom she shares with husband Andrew Lococo.

“Two babies on my lap,” she wrote in the caption, “our second little earthling joining us this autumn!!”

In the carousel, Bonnie is holding Elio as the two lay on the couch together. The toddler appears to kiss his mother’s stomach in one picture.

After sharing news of her growing family, Bonnie received a flood of well-wishes from fans and friends, including Harry Potter costar Evanna Lynch who commented, “Ahhh congratulations Bonnie!!!” Meanwhile, David Holmes, stunt double to Daniel Radcliffe in the movies, also shared the love, writing, “Congratulations, Bonnie.”

Bonnie, who played Ginny Weasley in the film franchise, welcomed her first child with Andrew in September 2023.

“We’re all healthy and happy,” she wrote in an Instagram announcement at the time. “Andrew and I are so in love with our sun! So grateful for our birth team that have held our hands throughout and made the journey so joyous and expanding.”

She shared that “birth is a wild experience” and thanked her medical team.

“Birth workers are amazing,” she continued. “Lastly thanks to Andrew my rock throughout birth quite literally as I squeezed onto you so tight and you never wavered. Elio has the most tender loving papa.”

Bonnie married Andrew in 2022, two years after the couple first sparked romance rumors. She shared the news in an Instagram post showing off her unique wedding ring, which featured a square blue stone.

Since becoming a mother, Bonnie, who runs the family outdoors club Earthling, has frequently posted from nature with Elio.

“These days have been spent getting closer to the land, watching Elio and Andrew sift compost and play in the garden," she said in a January post. She added: “Building trains and puzzles with Elio. Watching him grow into this determined, chatty, funny and busy little boy who always has time to stop for a kiss and a cuddle.”
