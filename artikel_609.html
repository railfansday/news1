50 Cent Says Diddy’s ‘Gonna Turn Up in This Next Joint’ After Prison Transfer

Combs was involved in a physical altercation behind bars earlier this week that resulted in him being hospitalized.

Diddy was reportedly transferred to a Pennsylvania federal prison following a physical altercation earlier this week, and 50 Cent wasted no time in trolling Combs on Friday (Oct. 16).

The G-Unit mogul has been busy in promo mode for his Street Fighter movie, which hit theaters on Friday, but he made sure to carve out a few seconds to stay updated on the Diddy saga behind bars.

“Diddy on the move, he gonna turn up in this next joint on gang,” he quipped on IG. The second slide had 50’s followers in stitches, as he posted a wind-up toy resembling Diddy that was blowing bubbles out of his butt. “Yoo this nicca the worst son nah lollll yall niccas stupid son,” Bobby Shmurda wrote.

TMZ was the first to report that Combs had been transferred from FCI Fort Dix in New Jersey to Federal Correctional Institution, Allenwood in Pennsylvania. Diddy was allegedly involved in a confrontation with another inmate that left him hospitalized with a head injury, but both Diddy‘s family and the Bureau of Prisons denied reports about the alleged dispute between the disgraced Bad Boy mogul and Christopher “Dudus” Coke, who is the leader of an infamous Jamaican crime organization.

A spokesperson for Diddy confirmed to Billboard that Combs was moved from Dix Hills to a “low-security facility.”

“We appreciate the Bureau of Prisons taking the safety and security of Sean and everyone involved seriously,” the spokesperson said in a statement. “His transfer from one low-security facility to another is an important distinction, and while we cannot speak to the BOP’s specific reasons for the move, it is consistent with a decision made with safety and security in mind. We are grateful for the attention being given to his well-being and hope he can continue serving his time without unnecessary disruption.”

50 Cent was behind last year’s Sean Combs: The Reckoning docuseries detailing Diddy’s case and history of alleged sexual abuse, which the Queens rapper won an Emmy for.

The “In Da Club” rapper appeared on Jimmy Kimmel Live! on Tuesday (Oct. 14) where he reacted to reports of Combs watching the series about his downfall in prison. “He saw the whole thing… I’m sure (he) thought it was the best documentary he ever saw in his life,” 50 said.

Combs was sentenced to 50 months behind bars in October 2025 after being convicted on two charges of prostitution transportation, which is a violation of the Mann Act. Diddy is currently scheduled to be released in March 2028.
